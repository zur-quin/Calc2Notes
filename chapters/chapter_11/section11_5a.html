11.5 Learning Objectives

The convergence tests we have looked at so far only apply to series with positive terms. In this section, we will focus on alternating series.

Warning

A common mistake students make at this point is to look at any series and say that the terms are decreasing to 0 so the series converges, however we know from the divergence test (section 11.2b) that this does not guarantee the series converges. This is only the case in the very special alternating series test.

The reasoning for why the alternating series test works is because the terms are decreasing in absolute value but alternating sign, we can always verify that the sequence of partial sums converges.
