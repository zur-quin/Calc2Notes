Remark

Geometric series are named this way because they often relate to proportions in geometric shapes. For example, consider ∑𝑛=1∞12𝑛.

∑𝑛=1∞12𝑛=12+14+18+116+…

In the limit, this series is equal to 1, which consists of the area of the entire square.

For any geometric series, we can determine the exact value if it converges.

∑𝑛=1∞𝑎𝑟𝑛−1=𝑎+𝑎𝑟+𝑎𝑟2+𝑎𝑟3+…𝑠𝑛=𝑎+𝑎𝑟+𝑎𝑟2+…+𝑎𝑟𝑛−1𝑟𝑠𝑛=𝑎𝑟+𝑎𝑟2+…+𝑎𝑟𝑛−1+𝑎𝑟𝑛𝑠𝑛−𝑟𝑠𝑛=𝑎−𝑎𝑟𝑛𝑠𝑛(1−𝑟)=𝑎(1−𝑟𝑛)𝑠𝑛=𝑎(1−𝑟𝑛)1−𝑟
